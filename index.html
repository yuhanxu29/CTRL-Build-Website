> FAQ

Getting started

What is CTRL Build?

A commerce hackathon where 150 to 200 students tackle real business challenges, build prototypes, and pitch to investors and industry leaders.

Who can participate?

University students in Ontario, any program and any year. Apply alone or with a team of up to four.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You can build with AI tools, no-code platforms, or traditional programming.

How is CTRL Build different from a traditional hackathon?

The focus is on business viability, creativity, customer needs, and execution.

How is it different from a case competition?

You build a working prototype that shows how your proposed solution works.

How much does it cost?

It's free to attend. Lunch and dinner are included.

Teams and building

How many people can be on a team?

Teams have three to four people.

What if I don't have a team?

Apply individually and we'll help you find teammates.

What will we be building?

You'll prototype a solution to a business challenge from a corporate or startup partner. It could be a website, an app, an AI-powered tool, or another digital product.

Can I use AI tools?

Yes. You're encouraged to use AI tools.

Can I start building beforehand?

You can get familiar with your tools in advance. Build your solution to the challenge during the event.

Will there be mentors?

Yes. Industry professionals, founders, and investors will give guidance and feedback throughout the day.

Competition and judging

What do I need to submit?

A prototype that demonstrates your solution and a short pitch deck explaining its business potential.

How will projects be judged?
  1. Is the problem real?
  2. Who is the customer?
  3. Does the business model work?
  4. Does the prototype show it?
  5. Can you pitch it and take questions?
Who will we pitch to?

A panel of venture capital investors, founders, and corporate representatives.

What can I win?

There are prizes for first, second, and third place, plus Best sponsor solution and People's choice. See the prizes section for details.

Event logistics

How long is CTRL Build?

10 hours in one day, 10:00 to 20:00.

What should I bring?

Bring a laptop, charger, and student ID.

Partnerships

Who is organizing CTRL Build?

RCEO, RCIG, and RCVC, three Rotman Commerce student groups.

How can my company get involved?

You can sponsor the event, provide a challenge, mentor, judge, or contribute in kind. Software credits and development resources are welcome. See the sponsorship packages.
