What is CTRL Build?

          
A hackathon for people who build businesses. Sponsors bring real problems from Toronto startups and companies. Teams of three or four pick one, build a prototype with whatever tools get them there, and pitch it to investors before the lights go out.

          
10 hoursone day, no all-nighter

Why you should come

    
Three reasons, hung where you can't miss them. Give them a push.

    

      
      

      

        
No code required
Build with no-code and AI tools. Business judgment is what gets scored, not lines of code.

        
Home by 20:00
Ten hours, start to finish. Lunch and dinner are on us, and you sleep in your own bed.

        
Real problems, real investors
Sponsors write the briefs. Investors hear the finalists. You leave with a portfolio piece and people who remember it.

Pick a track

    
Every challenge sits in one of these tracks. Choose the one that fits how you think, and build with a team that mixes programs.

    

      
Consumer and retail
Products people buy, subscriptions, marketplaces and the creator economy.

      
Future of work
Hiring, team tools, AI at work and new ways to earn outside a nine to five.

      
Fintech and payments
Money tools for students, small businesses and the people who serve them.

      
Social impact
Businesses that make Toronto better, from housing and food to access and care.

      
Open track
Any commerce problem that does not fit above. Pitch it and make the case.

RCEORotman Commerce Entrepreneurship Organization
RCEO empowers students to explore entrepreneurship through case competitions, networking events, workshops, and mentorship programs. The organization connects aspiring entrepreneurs with founders, industry leaders, and innovative companies to bridge the gap between education and real-world business experience.
rotmanceo.com

        
RCIGRotman Commerce Innovation Group
RCIG develops the next generation of innovators by providing students with hands-on consulting projects, professional development opportunities, and industry-focused events. The organization emphasizes creative problem-solving, business transformation, and meaningful social impact.
rotmancommerceinnovationgroup.org

        
RCVCRotman Commerce Venture Capital
RCVC introduces students to the venture capital and startup ecosystem through investment research, industry events, and experiential learning opportunities. The organization connects aspiring investors with venture capital firms, founders, and industry professionals to develop the next generation of venture capitalists.
rcventure.ca
